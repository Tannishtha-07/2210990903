## Project Overview

This research paper explores burnout among young adults, focusing on psychological causes, academic and workplace stress, emotional exhaustion, and coping mechanisms. The study aims to understand how modern lifestyle pressures impact mental well-being and proposes practical strategies for prevention and recovery.

---

## Objectives

- Study the major causes of burnout in young adults  
- Analyze emotional, academic, and workplace stress factors  
- Explore coping mechanisms and recovery strategies  
- Understand the impact of social expectations and digital culture on mental health
